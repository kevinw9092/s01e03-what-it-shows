How to tell them apart

  • parser: the tenant writes dates day first (every tenant on file does, en-GB and en-IE included) and the report came in by app, by email, or through a booking-v2 form. The shared parser reads the date month first, so day and month swap: 4 July becomes 7 April.
  • hint: the report came through the booking form on a booking-v3 tenant. That form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People type what it shows, month first; the form reads the date the tenant’s way, day first. The result looks like the parser bug, so it got merged with it, but the fix is in the form, not the parser.
  • shift: the date is one day off, not swapped. An invite one day early, every June booking a day off, an invite that arrived before the booking was made, a guest in Lisbon and a guest in Toronto shown different days for one meeting. Day/month order cannot do that; a time zone or offset can. The desk already noted these reports as a one-day move, then filed them under the tenant’s cause.

Ignore the intake tag when you sort these. It is guessed from the reporter’s browser locale, not from the tenant, and it disagrees with the tenant on many reports in this ticket.
